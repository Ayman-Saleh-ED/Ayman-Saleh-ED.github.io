About

I am moving from Process Instrumentation & Control Engineering into Data Science, and I bring the discipline of the first to the second.

Control engineering is the study of systems: inputs, feedback and the dynamics in between. That way of thinking is the foundation I now apply to data, modeling and analysis.

What drives me is a passion for complex technical systems, and it doesn’t stop at work. It carries into digital art and theorycrafting, where I take systems apart to understand exactly how they behave.
